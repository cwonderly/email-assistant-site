Email Assistant

A private, personal tool that helps one household clean up junk and subscription email in their own Gmail accounts.

What it does

  • Looks through the sender, subject and mailing-list details of email in the user's own Gmail accounts to find newsletters, ads and other bulk mail.
  • Shows the user a list of those senders on a page that only opens on their own computer. Nothing changes until the user picks senders and confirms.
  • For senders the user approves, it unsubscribes, moves their old marketing email to Gmail's Trash, and adds a Gmail filter for future marketing email. Order, shipping and account emails are kept.
  • If a sender only accepts unsubscribe requests by email, it writes that email and sends it only after the user approves it.

Who it's for

Email Assistant runs entirely on a single household's Mac, for that household's own email accounts. It isn't offered to the public, has no sign-up, and isn't available for download.

Privacy policy

Email Assistant uses the Gmail API. Its use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
